VEXN link tool

Reads Eporner and your site’s list of videos; writes nothing to the database.

One per line: a video, a playlist, a tag, a performer, a channel, a category, a search or an uploader. Videos already on the site are left out.

Paste a link and press Read links.
Each video becomes a card with its title, address, search title and descriptions ready to edit. Tick the ones to add, then make the SQL.
